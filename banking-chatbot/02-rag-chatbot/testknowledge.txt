ABHI Microfinance offers multiple loan products with competitive interest rates, easy repayment options, and quick processing.

Tractor Loan: A loan for farmers to purchase tractors, which are an essential necessity of farming.

Niswan Karza: A short to medium-term loan facility designed specifically for female entrepreneurs.

Light Commercial Vehicle Loan: A loan facility for business individuals involved in the transportation business.

Karobari Karza: A loan facility that helps businessmen raise capital for their businesses.

Kashtkar Karza: A loan that meets a farmer's financial needs to support expansion of their farming business.

Maweshi Karza: A lending product that caters to the needs of livestock farmers.

Sunheri Karza: Convert gold jewelry into working capital for business needs.

House Loan: A medium to long-term loan facility for house renovation and improvement purposes.

Motorcycle Loan: A short to medium-term loan facility to finance motorcycles of reputed brands.
